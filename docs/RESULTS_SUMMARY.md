# Results summary

A condensed version of the manuscript in `paper/` (see `paper/manuscript.pdf`).
Numbers are regenerated by the scripts named in brackets.

**Question.** Can an excitonic-insulator (EI) or exciton-condensate switch beat the
CMOS subthreshold limit, ln(10)k_BT/e (60 mV/decade at 300 K), at 300–350 K?

**Approach.** A chain of models. Each ingredient reproduces published numbers first
(`run_benchmarks.py`: 15 pass, 2 documented deviations).

| result | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| screening-limited ceiling | T_c ≤ Ry*/(4 r_s,c²) · 4m_r/(m_e+m_h) | `screening.py`, `tc_ceiling.py` |
| MoSe₂/WSe₂, parameters of Zeng & MacDonald (2020) | T_c ≤ 57 K (d = a_B ≈ 1.3 nm) | `tc_ceiling.py` |
| parameter box (m_r 0.05–0.6, ε 2–15, d 0.6–3 nm) | max 328 K at ε = 2; none ≥ 350 K; none ≥ 300 K for ε ≥ 4.5 | `tc_ceiling.py` |
| graphene double layer at the BiSFET point | US gap 31 meV; NS and SS screening → 0 | `dirac.py` |
| Ta₂NiSe₅ (Kaneko three-chain model) | 69–74% excitonic, needs lattice; T_c(MF)/2.2 = 298–377 K vs. measured 326 K | `ta2nise5_chain.py` |
| mean-field sub-CMOS swing | 60.4 mV/dec (MoSe₂/hBN/WSe₂, 350 K); 38.6 mV/dec (WTe₂, 300 K) | `fluctuations.py` |
| with Gaussian fluctuations | 102–140 and 137–280 mV/dec | `fluctuations.py` |
| with gate electrostatics (300 K) | ≥ 285 mV/dec | `real_gate.py` |
| switching energy vs. ideal CMOS, equal geometry | 2.2–45× higher | `energy.py` |
| disorder needed to keep T_c0 = 350 K | τ > 12 fs (not limiting) | `disorder.py` |
| ML surrogate (bootstrap ensemble) | R² 0.87, MAE 1.3 K; best design 117 K (ε ≥ 4.5), 233 K (ε ≈ 1.6) | `ml_v2.py` |

**Conclusion.** Room-temperature pairing is possible, but screening-limited 2D
condensation is not. The steep mean-field switching is removed by fluctuations.
No candidate beats CMOS at chip temperatures.

**Limitations.** Mean field plus Gaussian fluctuations; no intralayer exchange
(bracketed); two Ta₂NiSe₅ numbers deviate by 15–28%; no transport or circuit modelling.
